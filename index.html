Gridlit

Drop a block. Fill a line. Watch it light up and clear.

Get it on Google Play Block puzzle for Android

How it plays

  1. Drag

    You get three blocks at a time. Drag each one onto the 8×8 board, anywhere it fits.

  2. Fill

    Complete a row or a column and it lights up and clears, making room for more.

  3. Plan

    There is no timer. The round ends only when none of your blocks fits anywhere.

Clear several lines with one block, or keep clearing move after move, and the combo multiplies your score. Empty the whole board for a big bonus.

What’s in it

Daily challenge
The same blocks for everyone each day, with a score to beat. Beat it on consecutive days to build a streak.
Missions
Three at a time. Finish one and a harder one takes its place.
Boosters
A hammer, a fresh set of blocks and undo, for when you are stuck.
Eight themes
Neon, candy, pastel, ocean, sunset, gold and more, paid for with coins from clearing lines.
Colour-blind friendly
Optional symbols on every block colour.
No connection needed
The game runs offline, has no accounts and saves your round when you leave.

Free, with ads. A full-screen ad can appear between rounds, never during one. Other ads play only when you choose to watch one for a reward. A single purchase removes the full-screen ads.
